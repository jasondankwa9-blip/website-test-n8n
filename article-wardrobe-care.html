Wardrobes

Caring for Your Custom Wardrobe

A well-built wardrobe is made to last, and a little care keeps it looking its best for years. These simple habits will protect the finish, the fittings and the frame.

Dust and Clean Gently

Wipe surfaces with a soft, slightly damp cloth and dry straight after. Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive pads, which can dull the finish over time.

Keep It Dry and Cool

Wood does not like damp or direct heat. Keep the room ventilated, avoid placing the wardrobe against a wall that gets damp, and let clothes dry fully before storing them.

Look After Hinges and Runners

A tiny drop of light oil on hinges and drawer runners once in a while keeps doors swinging and drawers gliding smoothly. Tighten any loose screws before they wear the fitting.

Protect the Finish

Use handles rather than pushing on doors, and avoid dragging heavy items across surfaces. Small care like this keeps the finish looking new for many years.
