We Have Answers

Everything you need to know about our joinery and how we work. Still unsure? We're always a message away.

Booking a Project

How do I place an order?

Browse our gallery, find the pieces you love, then reach out on WhatsApp or Instagram with details of what you want, your preferred wood and finish, and any dimensions. We'll confirm and guide you through the rest.

Do you list prices on the website?

We don't list prices publicly as they vary based on size, materials and finish. Just message us on WhatsApp and we'll give you a full quote.

Can I customise a piece before ordering?

Yes. You can request specific dimensions, wood types, finishes and styling. Let us know your preferences when you reach out and we'll do our best to accommodate.

How do I know a design will suit my space?

Our team is happy to advise on what works for your room, style and budget. Describe what you're looking for and we'll guide you to the best option.

What do you build?

We build bespoke kitchens, fitted and freestanding wardrobes, bed frames, and one-off custom furniture. If you can picture it, message us and we'll tell you what we can do.

Craftsmanship & Builds

What's involved in a custom build?

Every project starts with your brief. We design, source solid materials, and hand-join and finish each piece in our workshop before delivery and installation.

Can you build any design?

In most cases, yes. Send us a photo or sketch of what you have in mind and we'll let you know what's possible and quote accordingly.

Do you offer an express service?

For urgent projects we may be able to offer express completion for an additional fee, depending on our workload. Message us to check availability.

How do I get started?

Drop us a message on WhatsApp or Instagram, or give us a call. We'll confirm a slot and take it from there.

Shipping & Delivery

Do you deliver outside Accra?

Yes. We deliver nationwide across Ghana. Delivery fees are paid separately by the customer and vary by location.

How will I know when my order is ready?

We'll notify you on WhatsApp as soon as your piece is ready. You'll never be left guessing.

How long does delivery take once it's ready?

Delivery timelines depend on your location. We'll give you an estimated timeframe once your order is dispatched.

Payments & Refunds

How do I pay?

We accept Mobile Money and bank transfer. A deposit is required upfront to confirm your booking and cover materials.

Can I get a refund if I change my mind?

Once materials are purchased or work has begun, deposits are non-refundable due to the bespoke nature of the work. Reach out as early as possible if something changes and we'll do our best to help.

Is it safe to pay a deposit upfront?

Yes. A deposit simply confirms your slot and lets us order materials and get started. We've served many happy clients across Ghana.
