5 Kitchen Layouts That Maximise Small Spaces

A small kitchen does not have to feel cramped. With the right layout and cabinetry built to fit your exact space, even the tightest room can work hard and look beautiful. Here are five layouts we use again and again.

1. The Galley Kitchen

Two runs of cabinets facing each other create an efficient corridor where everything is within reach. Ideal for narrow rooms, it keeps the cook, sink and stove in a tight, practical triangle.

2. The L-Shaped Kitchen

Cabinets along two adjoining walls free up the rest of the room for a small table or extra storage. It is one of the most flexible layouts for a compact home.

3. The U-Shaped Kitchen

Three walls of storage and worktop wrap around you, giving maximum cabinet space in a small footprint, perfect if you need to fit a lot into a little.

4. The Single-Wall Kitchen

Everything along one wall keeps the room open and airy. We build tall units right up to the ceiling so no vertical space is wasted.

5. The Island or Peninsula

Even a small kitchen can fit a slim island or a peninsula that doubles as prep space and a breakfast bar, adding storage and a place to gather without crowding the room.
