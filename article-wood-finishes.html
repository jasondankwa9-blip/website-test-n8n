Finishes

The Complete Guide to Choosing Wood Finishes

The finish you choose changes everything, how a piece catches the light, how it feels to the touch, and how it holds up to daily life. Here is a simple guide to the three finishes we offer most, and where each one works best.

High Gloss

A high gloss finish is bright, reflective and modern. It bounces light around a room, making small or dark spaces feel larger, and wipes clean in seconds, perfect for busy kitchens and statement wardrobes.

Semi-Gloss

Semi-gloss sits in the middle, a soft, satin sheen that is easy to clean without the mirror-like shine. It is our most versatile finish and suits almost any room in the home.

Matte

Matte is understated and contemporary, with no glare and a warm, natural feel. It hides fingerprints and light scratches well, making it a favourite for bedrooms and living spaces.

Which Finish for Which Room?

As a rule of thumb: high gloss for kitchens and anywhere you want light and easy cleaning, semi-gloss for pieces that move between rooms, and matte for calm, cosy spaces. Not sure? We will bring samples so you can see them in your own light before we build.
